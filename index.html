FAQ

Honest answers

Is it live now?

Private pilots are running now — that's what the free 2-week pilot is. Public self-serve launch comes after the pilot wave. Pilot customers get founding-customer pricing locked in forever.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You describe the bot in one sentence — in English, French, or Arabic. She asks the missing questions, builds it, and tests it. Your only job is approving the plan.

Which channels does it work on?

WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, email, and web chat — depending on your plan. The bot replies in your customers' language automatically.

What if the bot makes a mistake?

Anything sensitive — payments, deletions, angry customers — is escalated to you, never handled blindly. Every bot passes quality and safety gates before going live, and she self-heals when something breaks.

What happens after the free pilot?

You choose: keep the bot on a plan (from $29/mo), tweak it and keep going, or stop — we'll disconnect everything. Two weeks is enough to know if it earns its keep.
